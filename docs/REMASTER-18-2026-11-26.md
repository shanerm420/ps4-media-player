# The eighteen looks remastered — the brief, the gap, and the target

**Owner instruction, 2026-11-26, verbatim:**

> Those are all terrible and don't meet the new ceiling that we've discovered so
> they all have to be scrapped and completely remade with the same designs and
> mine but up to the new standards of the build and what it can do

This page is the specification that instruction needs. It is not a plan to
redesign: the designs are the owner's and they do not change. What changes is
what each one is built out of, so that it stands beside its design instead of
apologising for itself.

## 1. The bar

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What each look is judged against | its own design, side by side, at the same size |
| The budget | **50 ms at 1080p = 3 vsyncs** (G8), look box never below **1640x560** |
| The floor | a look that misses the budget is not finished even if it looks good, and a look that meets it is finished even if it could be prettier |
| What "finished" means | the design's elements are all present, lit and moving; nothing in the frame is a flat fill standing in for something |

Evidence of the gap: `files/round8/looks-2026-11-26/designs-vs-now.png`, every
design beside the frame that ships today, plus the eighteen frames themselves.

## 2. What the hardware allows the build to use

Do not specify a technique outside this list without a new measurement.

- **No custom shaders (R10).** Piglet ES2 runs only programs that ship as blobs
  in `libScePrecompiledShaders.sprx`. There is no GLSL compiler on the console
  and OpenOrbis ships none. The GPU path therefore buys *fixed* post-processing
  — bright-pass, blur, composite — and textured quads, not per-look shaders.
- **Render targets:** half-float works (`EXT_color_buffer_half_float` present);
  no 32-bit float targets, no float blending, no framebuffer fetch, no sRGB
  targets (M9). Warp-feedback is ping-pong FBOs.
- **Known-good on this console:** the bloom chain (BREW00102: five configs, all
  `SET` accepted, panel legible, bloom visible), one vsync per frame, timer
  queries present.
- **So the per-look work is mostly our rasterizer** — plates cached and cheap
  geometry are not the problem; the frame is spent in `flush` and in per-pixel
  conversion. The techniques that pay: cached world plates, depth layers, glow
  sprites stamped additively, lookup-table gradients, quarter-res soft layers,
  dirty regions, and a bloom composite.
- **The audio analysis is shared, not per look:** log-spaced bands, per-band
  beats with a ~150 ms refractory and geometric decay, packet interpolation,
  automatic gain (M1).

## 4. What every look needs, once, in shared code

1. **Plates and worlds** — one cached background per world, built off-frame and
   sliced (as `bg_build()` already is), so a world costs nothing per frame.
2. **Depth layers** — far/mid/near with parallax and a depth grade.
3. **Glow** — pre-rendered sprites in several sizes, stamped additively, plus a
   bright-pass and blur composite via the precompiled blobs where the GPU path
   is in use.
4. **Light** — a light pool, a rim light and a fog/haze scroll from a tileable
   tile at quarter resolution, upscaled.
5. **Particles** — one pooled system, per-look parameters, drawn as sprites.
6. **Grade** — LUT ramps for gradients, falloff and color, plus dither before
   the vignette.
7. **Dirty regions** — redraw and upload only what changed (A4).

## 5. Order of work

1. **The six empty frames** — 3 PULSE, 6 SCOPE, 9 NEON, 10 FLAG, 4 MIRROR,
   2 RADIAL. Worst gap, least geometry.
2. **The flagship** — 8 LIQUID.
3. **The crowd and stage family** — 11, 14, 15, 16.
4. **The rest** — 0, 1, 5, 7, 12, 13, 17.

Each look is finished only when: its design's elements are all present; it holds
50 ms at 1080p in three runs; its frame is captured beside its design; and a
ledger row names the numbers. A look that regresses another look's frame time is
not finished.

## 8. Out of scope, and why

- **Redesigning the looks.** The designs are the owner's; this replaces the
  implementation, not the art direction.
- **Custom shaders.** R10 stands until a measurement says otherwise.
- **Native 1080p for every look at 60 fps.** The audit already showed the
  software path cannot; the GPU path buys post-processing, not geometry. The
  ladder stays, and G8 is measured at whatever step the console settles on.
